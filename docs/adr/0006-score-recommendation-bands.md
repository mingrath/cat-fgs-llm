# Score recommendation bands

Status: accepted

The LINE OA prototype will map the 0-10 model-estimated FGS-style score to caregiver-facing recommendation bands. Concern begins at 4/10, roughly aligning with the published Feline Grimace Scale cutoff logic, while stronger veterinary-assessment guidance begins at 6/10.

## Bands

- **0-3**: no clear pain-related facial signs visible.
- **4-5**: possible pain-related facial signs; monitor closely and consider veterinary assessment if behavior has changed.
- **6-10**: stronger pain-related facial signs; veterinary assessment recommended.
- **Emergency signs**: recommend urgent veterinary care regardless of image score.

## Consequences

The score bands are prototype decision-support guidance, not diagnosis or treatment instruction. The system must not let a low score suppress emergency guidance when the caregiver reports severe warning signs.
