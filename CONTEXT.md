# Cat FGS Pain Detection

This context defines the domain language for a feline pain decision-support system based on photo-submitted facial evidence and Feline Grimace Scale concepts.

## Language

**Caregiver**:
A person who submits a cat photo to ask whether the cat may show signs consistent with pain.
_Avoid_: User, owner, customer

**Decision-support response**:
A cautious response that communicates whether the submitted photo is consistent with possible pain and whether veterinary assessment is recommended. It is not a diagnosis or analgesia instruction.
_Avoid_: Diagnosis, medical decision, treatment recommendation

**Feline Grimace Scale Action Unit**:
One of the facial components used by the Feline Grimace Scale to describe pain-related facial expression.
_Avoid_: Feature, landmark, facial part

**Vet anchor**:
A small independently scored reference set reviewed by a veterinarian and used to judge whether labels or model outputs are credible.
_Avoid_: Ground truth, gold labels

**Submitted photo**:
A caregiver-provided cat image captured outside the research dataset and used as the intake evidence for decision support.
_Avoid_: Dataset image, training image, sample

**Cannot-score case**:
A submitted photo where pain evidence should not be estimated because the face, pose, occlusion, image quality, or clinical context is insufficient.
_Avoid_: Low-confidence prediction, failed inference

**LINE intake**:
The caregiver-facing submission path through a LINE Official Account.
_Avoid_: App, chatbot, mobile app

**Research-grade model pipeline**:
A verifiable training, evaluation, calibration, and abstention pipeline intended to produce research evidence about feline pain detection. It is not a standalone clinical product.
_Avoid_: Product, app, deployed detector

**LINE OA prototype**:
A minimal LINE Official Account integration that proves a caregiver can submit a photo and receive a decision-support response from the research pipeline.
_Avoid_: Consumer product, production app

**Model-estimated FGS-style score**:
A 0-10 prototype score based on Feline Grimace Scale action-unit structure and generated by the model for a submitted photo. It must be paired with uncertainty, cannot-score handling, and a decision-support response.
_Avoid_: Diagnosis score, confirmed pain score, official clinical FGS result

**Concern guidance**:
A plain-language interpretation of the prototype score that tells the caregiver whether observation, caution, or veterinary assessment is recommended.
_Avoid_: Treatment advice, analgesia recommendation

**Clinic recommendation**:
A later-version feature that suggests veterinary clinics or animal hospitals based on caregiver location, available providers, and relevant specialty fit.
_Avoid_: Current scope, emergency dispatch, provider marketplace

**Action-unit subscore**:
A 0-2 model-estimated component score for one Feline Grimace Scale action unit shown as supporting detail below the total score. It is a component estimate, not a separate diagnosis.
_Avoid_: Muscle diagnosis, facial abnormality score

**Image-quality gate**:
A pre-score check that decides whether a submitted photo has enough visible facial evidence for a model-estimated FGS-style score. It should tolerate ordinary caregiver photo quality but reject images below a defined minimum standard.
_Avoid_: Perfect-photo requirement, hidden rejection

**Retake guidance**:
A plain-language instruction shown when a submitted photo cannot be scored, explaining how the caregiver should retake the photo.
_Avoid_: Error message, failure notice

**Borderline-quality photo**:
A submitted photo that passes the minimum image-quality gate but has visible limitations that reduce confidence in the model-estimated FGS-style score.
_Avoid_: Bad photo, unusable photo

**Low-confidence warning**:
A message shown with a score when the submitted photo is borderline quality or the model uncertainty is high, telling the caregiver that the estimate may be less reliable.
_Avoid_: Error, disclaimer-only result

**Recommendation band**:
A score range that maps a model-estimated FGS-style score to caregiver-facing concern guidance. Current bands are 0-3 no clear visible pain signal, 4-5 possible pain-related facial signs, and 6-10 stronger pain-related facial signs where veterinary assessment is recommended.
_Avoid_: Diagnosis category, treatment band

**Emergency override**:
A rule where severe clinical warning signs override the image score and trigger urgent veterinary guidance regardless of the model-estimated FGS-style score.
_Avoid_: Score exception, panic mode

**Image-first flow**:
The primary LINE OA interaction where a caregiver sends a submitted photo and receives a model-estimated FGS-style score, component estimates, and concern guidance without a required questionnaire.
_Avoid_: Questionnaire-first flow, intake form

**Conditional follow-up question**:
A question asked only when the score, image quality, uncertainty, or caregiver-requested guidance requires more context before giving safe concern guidance.
_Avoid_: Mandatory survey, full history taking

**Follow-up trigger**:
A rule that decides when LINE asks a conditional follow-up question. Current trigger: automatic one-question follow-up for model-estimated FGS-style scores 4-10, optional more-guidance follow-up for scores 0-3.
_Avoid_: Mandatory questionnaire, hidden triage logic

**Symptom check**:
A structured multi-select follow-up question used after scores 4-10 to add caregiver-observed context to the decision-support response.
_Avoid_: Free-text diagnosis interview, full clinical history

**Emergency symptom**:
A severe caregiver-reported sign such as breathing difficulty, collapse, bleeding, seizure, or inability to urinate that triggers urgent veterinary guidance regardless of score.
_Avoid_: High score symptom, model finding

**Thai-first UI**:
A caregiver-facing LINE OA interface where primary messages, warnings, retake guidance, score interpretation, and recommendations are written in natural Thai. Internal research labels, metrics, configs, and logs may remain English.
_Avoid_: English-first UI, literal translation

**Consented research dataset**:
A stored collection of submitted photos and derived annotations that caregivers have explicitly allowed to be used for research and model improvement.
_Avoid_: Silent collection, default storage, scraped user data

**Derived annotation**:
A machine-generated or human-reviewed label, segmentation mask, crop, quality state, action-unit estimate, or metadata record produced from a submitted photo for research and model improvement.
_Avoid_: Raw result only, hidden label

**Research storage consent**:
A caregiver permission step that explains what data will be stored, why it is stored, how long it may be retained, and how it may be used for research or model improvement.
_Avoid_: Implied consent, vague agreement

**Optional research consent**:
A consent pattern where the caregiver can receive a prototype result without agreeing to long-term research storage, while being clearly invited to contribute the photo and derived annotations to improve the model.
_Avoid_: Forced consent, bundled consent

**Pseudonymous LINE identity**:
A non-raw identifier derived from LINE user/session identity and stored with consented research records to support grouping, leakage control, and repeated-submission analysis without storing raw personal identity.
_Avoid_: Raw LINE user ID, real-name identity

**Submission record**:
A durable research record for one consented submitted photo and its derived annotations, linked to consent version, model version, quality state, and pseudonymous identity.
_Avoid_: Loose image file, unlabeled upload

**Minimum-friction data collection**:
A LINE OA data-collection approach that asks only for information needed to deliver immediate caregiver value or safety guidance, avoiding up-front forms that reduce usage.
_Avoid_: Intake form, research-first questionnaire

**Progressive profiling**:
A pattern where optional dataset fields such as cat identity are collected gradually when they naturally fit the current user flow, such as symptom follow-up or repeated submissions.
_Avoid_: Mandatory profile setup, registration flow

**Cat identity prompt**:
An optional progressive-profiling prompt that asks whether a consented or repeated submission belongs to the same cat, a new cat, or should be skipped.
_Avoid_: Required cat profile, pre-score registration

**Roboflow dataset workspace**:
An external computer-vision workspace used to store consented images, annotations, batches, dataset versions, and selected training runs. It should receive pseudonymous submission records from the LINE backend rather than raw LINE identity.
_Avoid_: Consent source of truth, raw upload dump

**Dataset sync boundary**:
The boundary between the LINE backend as the consent/provenance system of record and Roboflow as the annotation, dataset management, and training workspace.
_Avoid_: One-system shortcut, unmanaged export
